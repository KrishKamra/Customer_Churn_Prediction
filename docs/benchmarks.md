## Protocol

* **Data:** IBM Telco Customer Churn, 5,634 training / 1,409 holdout customers (stratified, seed `42`).
* **Validation:** Repeated Stratified 5-Fold CV × 2 repeats on the training split. Every fold re-fits the full feature pipeline *and* isotonic calibration, so no information crosses folds.
* **Selection:** highest mean CV PR-AUC (ties broken by lower expected cost).
* **Decision threshold:** minimises `5×FN + 1×FP` on out-of-fold probabilities; the holdout is never used for any choice.
* **Dollar figures:** assume 1 cost unit = $50 (one wasted retention offer); a missed churner costs 5 units ($250). These are configurable assumptions (`CHURNAI_COST_*`), not measured values.
